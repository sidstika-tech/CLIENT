◑ SETUP GUIDE
Step 1 — Create your Meta Developer App (one time)
1. Go to developers.facebook.com → Create App → Business type
2. Add products: Messenger, Instagram Graph API, WhatsApp Business
3. Copy your App ID and App Secret
4. Add OAuth redirect URI:
5. Submit for App Review to request: pages_messaging, instagram_manage_messages, instagram_content_publish
Step 2 — Add env vars to your Vercel project
META_APP_ID=your_app_id
META_APP_SECRET=your_app_secret
META_WEBHOOK_VERIFY_TOKEN=doubleeight_webhook_2024
SERVER_URL=https://api.doubleeight.online
CLIENT_URL=https://doubleeight.online
Step 3 — Configure webhook in Meta App Dashboard
Webhook URL: Verify Token: doubleeight_webhook_2024
Subscribe to fields: messages, messaging_postbacks, feed
Step 4 — Connect your accounts
Once env vars are set and deployed, click the "Connect" buttons above.
A Meta popup will open → you log in and approve → your AI starts working automatically.

Instagram + Facebook: Needs an Instagram Business account linked to a Facebook Page.
WhatsApp: Needs a WhatsApp Business API number (apply at business.facebook.com).
